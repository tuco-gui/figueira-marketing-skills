# QA — Final Scorecard

Score 0–2 per item:
- 0 = fails/materially missing
- 1 = acceptable but needs refinement
- 2 = strong/consistent

## Strategy
- clear primary job
- decision hierarchy
- real content
- alignment with audience

## Brand
- coherence with identity
- visual specificity
- intentional typography
- coherent imagery/icons

## UX
- navigation/flow
- CTA/actions
- forms
- feedback and states
- mobile

## Craft
- grid/alignment
- spacing
- contrast
- component consistency
- motion

## Technical quality
- basic accessibility
- perceived performance
- semantics
- SEO when applicable
- tracking when applicable

## Acceptance rules

- no critical item at 0;
- `brand > visual specificity` cannot be 0 in a redesign/new page;
- `UX > mobile` cannot be 0 in a public experience;
- browser review must be recorded as `EXECUTED` or `NOT EXECUTED`;
- data/proof must be verified or explicitly flagged.

Do not turn the score into false precision. Use it to find flaws, not to claim scientific quality.
