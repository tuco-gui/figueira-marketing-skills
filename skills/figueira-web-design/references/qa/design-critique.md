# QA — Design Critique

Run the critique through five lenses.

## 1. Strategy
- is the primary job evident?
- does the hierarchy favor the right action?
- do design and content serve the same argument?

## 2. Identity
- does it look like that brand?
- are there decisions specific to the industry/subject?
- is the visual expression coherent with the positioning?

## 3. Composition
- is there rhythm?
- is the contrast between sections intentional?
- are alignments consistent?
- do whitespace and density aid reading?

## 4. Interface
- are states clear?
- do actions look like actions?
- do similar components behave the same way?
- does mobile preserve priority?

## 5. Craft
- is the typography refined?
- do images have consistent treatment?
- are details aligned?
- does motion have a function?
- are there elements that could be removed without loss?

## Output

List at most:
- 3 critical problems;
- 5 relevant improvements;
- 3 strong elements that should be preserved.

Prioritize impact, not comment count.
