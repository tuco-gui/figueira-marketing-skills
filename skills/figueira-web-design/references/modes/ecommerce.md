# Mode — E-commerce

## Job

Help the buyer discover, evaluate, trust, choose and complete the purchase with the least friction compatible with a conscious decision.

## Experience system

Think of the whole system, not just the homepage:
- home/merchandising;
- navigation and search;
- category/PLP;
- filters and sorting;
- product card;
- PDP;
- variants;
- stock/lead time;
- cart;
- checkout;
- confirmation;
- account/orders;
- empty/error states.

## Home

The homepage should orient discovery and position the store. Do not turn it into a generic landing page with dozens of institutional sections.

Prioritize based on catalog and strategy:
- categories;
- collections;
- new arrivals;
- best sellers only if backed by real data;
- purchase benefits;
- editorial content;
- real seasonal merchandising.

## PLP

- give category context;
- preserve enough density for comparison;
- filters should match the attributes people actually use;
- do not hide important sorting;
- cards need to prioritize identification, price, variation and action according to the purchase model;
- do not turn every card into a mini landing page.

## PDP

The PDP is a decisive surface. Ensure:
- clear name and identification;
- adequate media;
- true price/condition;
- understandable variants;
- real availability;
- dominant CTA;
- delivery/shipping time when possible;
- benefits and specifications;
- relevant policy/exchange info;
- reviews only when real;
- related products with justifiable logic.

## Cart and checkout

- reduce distraction without hiding relevant information;
- clear total cost and conditions;
- simple quantity editing;
- actionable errors;
- checkout compatible with autofill and mobile;
- no fake urgency;
- no deceptively pre-selected paid add-ons.

## Mobile commerce

Prioritize:
- thumb reach;
- sticky CTA when it helps and doesn't cover content;
- functional gallery;
- usable filters;
- appropriate keyboards in forms;
- tap targets;
- an understandable order summary.

## Possible metrics

Only when data/scope exist:
- conversion;
- add-to-cart;
- checkout completion;
- abandonment;
- AOV/ticket;
- revenue per visitor;
- speed/Core Web Vitals;
- no-result searches;
- filter usage.

Do not turn a reference example into a claimed client result.
