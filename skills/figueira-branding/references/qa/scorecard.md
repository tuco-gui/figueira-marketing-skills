# QA: Final scorecard (Gate 11)

## Objective
Final check before handoff (Gate 12) — ensure the brand package is coherent, honest about what is evidence vs. hypothesis, and usable.

## Dimensions evaluated

### 1. Strategic coherence
- Is the positioning (Gate 3) reflected in verbal (Gate 5) and visual (Gate 8) identity?
- Does any piece of the system contradict the defined strategy?

### 2. Evidence honesty
- Is every market/perception/social-proof claim correctly classified (`VERIFIED FACT`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `NO DATA`, etc., per `research.md`)?
- Was any research or data invented?

### 3. Real differentiation
- Does the winning territory pass the `anti-ai-slop.md` test?
- Is the brand distinguishable from the direct competitors identified in research?

### 4. System completeness
- Are logo, color, typography, voice and applications all documented in `brand-guidelines.md`?
- Do the applications tested in Gate 9 cover the business's real touchpoints (not just conceptual mockups)?

### 5. Governance readiness
- Is the rollout plan (`rollout-governance.md`) defined?
- Have the mandatory approvals been identified, and was none executed without explicit approval?

### 6. Mode/depth consistency
- Does the deliverable match the minimum expected for the mode used (`references/modes/<mode>.md`) and the depth level (`LEAN`/`STANDARD`/`DEEP`) agreed at the start of the project?

## Output format
For each dimension: pass / pass with caveat / fail — with a note on what's missing if it doesn't pass. A project only moves to Gate 12 (handoff) once every dimension is "pass" or "pass with caveat" explicitly accepted by the owner.

## Common mistake to avoid
Marking everything as "pass" without a real check just to close the project faster — the scorecard exists precisely to catch this before handoff, not after the brand is already in production.
