# QA: Anti-AI-slop (used in Gate 7 and throughout)

## Objective
Detect and eliminate the generic patterns that make a brand look "AI-generated" — not because AI is bad, but because these patterns signal the absence of real strategic decision-making.

## Visual patterns to avoid by default
- Generic purple/blue "tech startup" gradient.
- Decorative organic blob with no relation to the concept.
- "Spark"/four-point star icon as a symbol of "AI innovation."
- Mesh gradient as a background fill with no rationale.
- Geometric sans-serif typography as an automatic default for "modern" (Poppins/Montserrat-like without justification).
- Symmetrical centered composition with excessive "premium" empty space unrelated to the strategy.

## Verbal patterns to avoid by default
- "Revolutionary," "disruptive," "transformative," "innovative" without concrete proof behind them.
- "We elevate," "we reimagine," "we reinvent" as the opening of a positioning statement.
- Stacked adjectives without substance: "authentic, human, close, trustworthy, innovative."
- A generic tagline that could serve any company in the sector ("The future is now," "Connecting people and ideas").
- Empty personification of purpose ("we exist to change the world") without real proof of capacity.

## How to use this checklist
It's not a list of absolute prohibitions — it's a warning sign. If a territory or piece of text uses one of these patterns, ask: is this here because it's the right choice for this specific brand, or because it was the path of least resistance? If there's no defensible answer, discard or replace it.
