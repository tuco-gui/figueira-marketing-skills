# QA: Critique and selection (Gate 7)

## Objective
Evaluate the three visual territories from Gate 6 against explicit criteria, so the final choice is defensible — not just "I like this one better" with no rationale.

## Evaluation criteria, per territory
1. **Strategic alignment** — does the territory visually express the positioning from Gate 3? Or is it pretty but disconnected from strategy?
2. **Category differentiation** — does the territory stand apart from the saturated visual codes identified in research (`research.md`)? Or does it repeat what the category already does?
3. **Distinction among the three territories** — apply the genuineness test from `visual-territories.md`.
4. **Resistance to AI slop** — check against `anti-ai-slop.md`.
5. **System viability** — can the concept become a full logo/identity system (Gate 8), or is it an idea that only works as a single piece?
6. **Durability** — does the territory have staying power, or does it depend on a passing aesthetic that will date quickly?
7. **Proof/traceability** — do the color/typography/composition decisions have a recorded rationale, or were they arbitrary choices?

## Output format
For each territory: a qualitative note per criterion (doesn't need to be numeric) + final verdict (advance/discard/combine elements with another territory). If the decision is to combine elements from two territories, explicitly document what was absorbed from each — don't present the combination as if it were a fourth territory that appeared out of nowhere.

## Who decides
The skill presents the comparative analysis; the final choice of territory (or combination) needs validation from the owner before moving to Gate 8 — visual territories are not on `SKILL.md`'s mandatory approval list, but locking this in avoids costly rework in later gates.

## Output of this gate
One territory (or documented combination) selected, with a recorded rationale, ready for Gate 8.
