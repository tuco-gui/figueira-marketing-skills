# Phase: Governance and rollout (part of Gate 10)

## Objective
Define how the brand goes from "approved document" to "in use in the real world" without breaking consistency, catching the team off guard, or executing any irreversible action without explicit approval.

## What to resolve
1. **Rollout sequence** — what changes first (site, social media, sales materials, email signature, physical packaging) and why in that order.
2. **Technical dependencies** — domain, hosting, DNS, social media accounts, systems that reference the old brand (makes it easier to find everything that needs to change).
3. **Communication** — who needs to be informed before launch (internal team, customers, partners) and in what order.
4. **Legacy assets** — what happens to old materials/domains/profiles (redirect, archive, keep running in parallel during a transition period).
5. **Point of no return** — from which action the change becomes public and hard to reverse (main site launch, public post, customer announcement). Everything before this is reversible; everything after requires that rollback also be planned.

## Approval rule (non-negotiable)
Publishing a new brand, launch/relaunch, public change of name/logo, production changes, permanent change of domain/handle, deploy, permanent deletion of assets, external communication — all of these require explicit approval from the owner before execution. This holds even if `SKILL.md` has already been approved as a process — every real execution action needs its own approval.

## Minimum checklist before any rollout action
- Has the owner explicitly approved this specific action (not just the project in general)?
- Is there a rollback plan if something goes wrong?
- Have the technical dependencies been mapped?
- Has the necessary internal/external communication already happened or been scheduled?

## Output
A sequenced rollout plan + governance checklist, ready to guide the actual execution (which remains outside this skill's autonomous decision scope).
