# Phase: Three visual territories (Gate 6)

## Objective
Generate three genuinely different visual directions — all coherent with the same strategy — before converging on any final logo or locked palette. This exists to avoid premature anchoring on the first idea and to give the owner a real choice, not the illusion of one.

## What each territory needs to contain
1. **Concept** — the central idea in one sentence.
2. **Strategic rationale** — why this territory serves the positioning defined in Gate 3.
3. **Composition** — how elements organize in space.
4. **Typography** — family(ies), weight, character.
5. **Color** — palette with rationale (not "because it's pretty").
6. **Imagery/photography** — style, if applicable to the category.
7. **Graphic elements** — patterns, textures, supporting graphic elements.
8. **Motion** — when relevant to the business (digital-first, social, product with an interface).
9. **Logo direction** — direction of the symbol/logotype, without being polished final art.
10. **Demonstration applications** — 2-3 mockups showing the territory in real use, identified as mockups.
11. **Main risk** — what could go wrong with this territory (e.g., "could age fast," "could be confused with competitor X").
12. **What differentiates it from the other two** — in one sentence, what only this territory has.

## Genuineness test
Before presenting, ask: if I remove the logo/name, can someone tell the three territories apart just by composition, color and typography? If not, they're variations of a single idea, not three territories — this is forbidden by `SKILL.md`.

## How to present
When browser/Figma/rendering capability is available, lay all three side by side for direct visual comparison — a decision becomes much clearer in simultaneous comparison than across three separate documents.

## Visual creation rules (from SKILL.md, reinforced here)
- Do not start with a palette or a logo — start with concept and composition.
- Do not force minimalism, brutalism, luxury, editorial or tech without a relation to the real strategy.
- Avoid the cliché pairs: premium = serif+beige+gold; technology = blue/purple+gradient+geometric sans; sustainable = green+leaf; "faith-based" = cross/fish/gold. If a territory lands on one of these defaults, ask whether it's intentional and defensible or just the path of least resistance.

## Output of this gate
Three complete territories, ready for Gate 7 (critique and selection).
