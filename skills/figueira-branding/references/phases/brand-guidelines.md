# Phase: Brand guidelines (part of Gate 10)

## Objective
Document the brand system so that another person — inside or outside the team — can apply it correctly without needing to ask whoever created it.

## Recommended document structure
1. **Fundamentals** — positioning (summary, not the full Gate 3 document), personality, voice (summary of `verbal-identity.md`).
2. **Logo** — the full Gate 8 system: versions, clear space, minimum size, what not to do.
3. **Color** — palette with codes and usage rationale.
4. **Typography** — families, hierarchy, fallback.
5. **Graphic elements and supporting assets** — how and when to use them.
6. **Photography/imagery** — style, treatment, what to avoid.
7. **Reference applications** — the examples validated in Gate 9 (not the conceptual mockups from Gate 6).
8. **Tone of voice and copy examples** — pulled from `verbal-identity.md`, with real examples.
9. **Common mistakes and what not to do** — consolidated from all previous gates.
10. **Contact/governance** — who approves exceptions, where the source files live.

## Usefulness principle
A guideline nobody can follow without help hasn't done its job. Prefer concrete examples ("like this," "not like this") over long descriptive text. Every rule should be verifiable — someone looking at a finished piece should be able to say whether it follows or breaks the rule.

## Level of detail by depth
- `LEAN` — a lean document covering logo, color, typography and 2-3 usage rules; no extensive governance section.
- `STANDARD` — the full structure above.
- `DEEP` — includes variations by channel/touchpoint, advanced use cases, and technical appendices (file specs, font licenses).

## Output
A versionable brand guidelines document, ready to be used as the source of truth for any future application of the brand.
