# Testing — Variants & A/B

Every variant should represent a clear hypothesis.

Examples:
- outcome vs. pain;
- proof vs. mechanism;
- consultative vs. direct CTA;
- short vs. explanatory hero;
- early vs. late social proof.

Record:
- hypothesis;
- variable;
- audience;
- channel;
- primary metric;
- guardrail metrics;
- start/end date;
- result;
- decision.

Don't declare a winner with an insufficient sample just because it "looks better."
