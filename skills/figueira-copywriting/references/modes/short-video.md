# Mode — Short Video

The script should be written for speech and editing, not as an article.

## Structure
1. 0-2s: a clear, specific hook.
2. 2-8s: confirm relevance and open the tension.
3. Body: fast progression, examples and logical cuts.
4. Payoff: deliver what the hook promised.
5. CTA: proportional to the awareness level.

Use pattern interrupts and open loops sparingly.

Do not optimize retention with a deceptive promise.
