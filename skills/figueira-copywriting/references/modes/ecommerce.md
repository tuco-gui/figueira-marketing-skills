# Mode — E-commerce

Copy should reduce uncertainty and increase desire without hiding material conditions.

## PDP
- product name and promise;
- priority benefits;
- important specifications;
- verifiable visual/social proof;
- use case and occasion;
- size/material/care/warranty/shipping when relevant;
- objections;
- CTA.

## Category/collection
- purchase intent;
- difference between lines;
- guidance for choosing;
- SEO without compromising readability.

## Cart/checkout
- trust microcopy;
- clear shipping/timing/price;
- friction reduction;
- coherent upsell/cross-sell, no dark patterns.

Do not invent reviews, stock levels, buyer counts, or urgency.
