# Strategy — Funnel & Message Match

Copy should continue the conversation started at the previous touchpoint.

Map:
- source;
- prior promise;
- prior knowledge;
- intent;
- next action.

## TOF
Educate, generate identification, curiosity and relevance.

## MOF
Differentiate, explain the mechanism, demonstrate value and build trust.

## BOF
Address objections, proof, offer, risk and the decision.

Avoid an ad about "saving 50%" that leads to a page whose hero only talks about "innovation for your business."
