# Strategy — Big Idea & Mechanism

## Big Idea
A central thesis that organizes the piece and makes the reader see the problem/opportunity from a useful angle.

Criteria:
- relevant;
- simple;
- distinct;
- defensible;
- connected to the offer.

## Mechanism
Explain:
- why the problem occurs;
- why common solutions fail;
- why this approach can work.

A mechanism should increase understanding and belief, not create pseudoscience.
