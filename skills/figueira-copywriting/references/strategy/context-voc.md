# Strategy — Context & VOC

## Rule
Before asking, exhaust the existing context.

Priority sources:
- briefs/forms;
- calls/meetings;
- CRM/WhatsApp;
- research;
- verifiable reviews;
- tickets/support;
- analytics;
- sales materials;
- competitor research.

## VOC extraction
Organize real quotes by:
- pains;
- desires;
- objections;
- purchase triggers;
- alternatives already tried;
- vocabulary;
- fears;
- decision criteria;
- desired outcomes.

Don't "improve" the customer's words before recording the original.

Then synthesize themes without pretending the synthesis is a literal quote.
