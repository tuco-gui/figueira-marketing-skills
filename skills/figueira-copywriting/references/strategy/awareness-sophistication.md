# Strategy — Awareness & Sophistication

## Awareness
- Unaware: create recognition/insight.
- Problem-aware: deepen the cause/cost and show that a way out exists.
- Solution-aware: differentiate categories/methods.
- Product-aware: strengthen proof, objections and offer.
- Most aware: conditions, real urgency and CTA can dominate.

## Sophistication
Ask:
- How many similar promises already exist?
- Is the audience skeptical?
- Does everyone use the same words?
- Has the market already "burned" certain claims?

The higher the sophistication, the more it should rely on mechanism, proof, demonstration and differentiation — not bigger adjectives.
