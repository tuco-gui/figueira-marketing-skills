# Persuasion — Objections & Risk

Map objections by category:
- need;
- efficacy;
- trust;
- differentiation;
- price;
- effort;
- time;
- integration/compatibility;
- risk;
- support;
- implementation.

Address an objection with:
- proof;
- explanation;
- demonstration;
- process;
- a real guarantee;
- transparency;
- a fair comparison.

Do not belittle the buyer's fear.
