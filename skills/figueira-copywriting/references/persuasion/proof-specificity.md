# Persuasion — Proof & Specificity

Rough hierarchy of strength:
1. observable demonstration;
2. verifiable data;
3. verifiable case study;
4. verifiable testimonial;
5. relevant credential/authority;
6. plausible explanation/mechanism;
7. the seller's own claim.

Prefer specific over vague.

"We cut closing time from X to Y" is stronger than "we optimized sales" — only if X and Y are verifiable.
