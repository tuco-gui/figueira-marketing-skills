# Persuasion — Authority & Social Proof

Use only real, relevant signals:
- certifications;
- experience;
- experts;
- customers;
- reviews;
- volume;
- case studies;
- media coverage;
- community.

Do not use:
- logos without authorization/context;
- "over X customers" with no source;
- an invented testimonial;
- borrowed authority with no material connection to the offer.
