# QA — CRO / Copy Chief

The function is to challenge the piece like a conversion-minded editor.

## Questions
- Is there ONE dominant message?
- Does the hero deliver immediate relevance?
- Does the argument follow a logical order?
- Is there a redundant section?
- Does proof appear early enough?
- Does the reader know what to do?
- Is the cognitive cost high?
- Is the CTA coherent with intent?
- Do the copy and the intended design compete with each other?
- Is the offer clear?

Don't micro-edit before fixing the macro-structure.
