# QA — Persuasion Audit

Audit before rewriting.

Questions:
- What is the explicit and implicit objective?
- Does the text demonstrate an understanding of the reader?
- Is the value clear?
- Is there a reason to believe?
- Were the central objections addressed?
- Is there friction or a defensive tone?
- Does the CTA feel like the logical next step?
- Was the brand voice preserved?

Prioritize the 3-5 highest-leverage fixes before polishing microcopy.
