# QA — Anti AI Slop

## Detect
- corporate clichés;
- empty superlatives;
- overly symmetrical sentences;
- repetitive AI-style structure;
- excessive "it's not X, it's Y";
- an artificial sequence of very short sentences;
- too many rhetorical questions;
- too many emojis/exclamation points;
- growth jargon with no substance;
- mixed metaphors;
- a generic CTA;
- a headline interchangeable with a competitor's.

## Fix
- go back to VOC;
- add a concrete detail;
- cut adjectives;
- insert proof;
- vary the rhythm;
- use language consistent with the brand.
