# QA — Compliance

Compliance depends on industry, platform and jurisdiction.

## Minimum checklist
- is the claim regulated?
- is the guarantee permitted?
- is the competitive comparison defensible?
- does the testimonial require disclosure?
- is the promotional condition clear?
- are price and installment terms correct?
- is there a sensitive financial/health/outcome promise?
- does the platform's policy restrict the language?

If the topic is regulated or time-sensitive, verify the current source before publishing.

Compliance does not replace legal review when it's needed.
