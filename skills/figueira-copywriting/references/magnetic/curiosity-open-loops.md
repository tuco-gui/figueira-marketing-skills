# Magnetic — Curiosity & Open Loops

Useful curiosity creates a genuine question in the reader's mind.

## Rules
- only open loops that will be closed;
- close small loops quickly;
- long loops need to justify the attention time;
- don't hide essential information just to force a click;
- don't use "secret" without real substance.

Useful question: "Will the reader be satisfied when the loop closes?"
