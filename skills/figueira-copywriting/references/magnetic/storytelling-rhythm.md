# Magnetic — Storytelling & Rhythm

## Narrative levers
- curiosity;
- emotional mirror;
- goal;
- obstacle;
- contrast;
- surprise;
- payoff.

## Rhythm
Alternate:
- a short, impactful sentence;
- expansion;
- proof/example;
- kicker/transition.

Avoid a robotic cadence of 3-5 word sentences in every paragraph.

Story serves the argument; it is not decoration.
