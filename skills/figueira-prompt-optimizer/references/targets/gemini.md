# Gemini adapter

For text/coding, use clear natural-language instructions with explicit context, constraints and output expectations. Do not repeat content already present in the relevant `GEMINI.md` hierarchy unless necessary.

## Gemini Image
Emphasize image/shot type, subject, setting, lighting, camera angle, lens/focus when useful, composition/style and aspect ratio when relevant.

For edits, state the requested change and what must remain unchanged.

## Gemini/Veo Video
Use subject/action, camera angle, camera movement, lens/effects when useful, lighting, mood/style/ambiance and audio/dialogue when supported and requested.

When a negative field is supported, prefer listing unwanted elements rather than verbose “do not” instructions inside the positive prompt.
