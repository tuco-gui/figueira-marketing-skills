# Runway adapter

## Image
Use descriptive visual language rather than conversational instructions. Consider subject, scene, composition, lighting, color, style, focus, angle, text and mood. Prefer positive phrasing.

## Video from image
Keep prompts simple and motion-focused. Prioritize subject motion, camera motion, scene motion and style descriptors only when needed.

The source image already establishes much of the visual composition and style. Do not redundantly rebuild it in text. Prefer positive descriptions over negative prompting.
