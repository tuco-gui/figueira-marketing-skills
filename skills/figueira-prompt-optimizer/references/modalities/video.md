# Video modality

## Text-to-video
Consider subject/action, environment, camera framing/movement, subject motion, scene motion, temporal progression, lighting/mood, visual style, duration/aspect ratio and audio/dialogue only where supported.

## Image-to-video
The source image already defines much of subject, composition, color, lighting and style. Focus on what moves, how it moves, camera movement, environmental motion, pacing, temporal transition and what must remain stable.

Avoid re-describing the entire source image.

## Video edit / transformation
Separate **Modify** from **Preserve**.

## Reference-to-prompt
First infer observable beats, camera behavior, action, environment and style. Then adapt that neutral description to the requested generator.
